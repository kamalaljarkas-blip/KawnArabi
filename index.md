# KawnArabi ContentFactory

A private production tool operated by KawnArabi for creating and publishing Arabic
documentary videos to the owner's own social accounts.

The application integrates with TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.
It is used by its owner only and does not accept third-party registrations.
