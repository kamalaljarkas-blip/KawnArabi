# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

## 1. Who we are

ContentFactory is a private production tool operated by KawnArabi for its own video publishing.

## 2. What we collect

- TikTok account authorization token (stored locally on the operator's own server).
- The operator's TikTok display name and avatar, used only to show account status.

We do not collect data from any other TikTok user.

## 3. How we use it

Solely to upload videos to the operator's own TikTok account at the operator's request.

## 4. Where it is stored

On the operator's private server. It is never transmitted to any third party other than
TikTok's official API endpoints.

## 5. Retention

The token is kept until the operator revokes access or deletes it.

## 6. Your controls

The operator may revoke the App at any time from TikTok: Settings → Security & Permissions →
Manage app permissions.

## 7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.
